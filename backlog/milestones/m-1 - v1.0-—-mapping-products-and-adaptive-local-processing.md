## Description

Build on the accepted V0 `SparseScene v1` and verified cache foundation to add
local-coordinate-to-CRS georeferencing, dense reconstruction, DSM/DTM and
orthomosaic products, declarative/adaptive recipes, product pruning, and
resource-aware single-machine scheduling. These capabilities are explicitly
post-V0 and must consume named artifact contracts rather than weakening ADR-012.
