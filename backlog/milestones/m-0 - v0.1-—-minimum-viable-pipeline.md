## Description

Turn the linked scaffold into a pipeline that produces real products: ingest a drone dataset, reconstruct it with COLMAP, georeference it, raster a DSM and an orthomosaic, and drive the whole thing from a cached DAG. The exit demo is `nadir process ./images` writing a georeferenced COG instead of exiting 1.
